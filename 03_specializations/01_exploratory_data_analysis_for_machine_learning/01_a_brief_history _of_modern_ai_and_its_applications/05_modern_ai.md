# Modern AI

**Two major areas of rapid growth and innovation:**
  - **Computer Vision:**  
    - Self-driving cars
    - Medical imaging (x-rays, MRIs) for diagnosis—machines are approaching or surpassing expert-level performance
  - **Natural Language Processing (NLP):**  
    - Translation, sentiment analysis, news clustering, automated writing, and more

## Why Is This Era Different?

- **Bigger Datasets:**  
  - Availability of large, diverse datasets (e.g., ImageNet) enables models to learn complex patterns across many fields.
  - Cloud infrastructure allows for cheap, scalable data storage and collection.

- **Faster Computers:**  
  - Powerful, affordable hardware for processing and storing data.
  - Modern laptops outperform the expensive mainframes of the 1980s.

- **Deep Learning Innovation:**  
  - Neural networks have become practical and successful, with constant innovation leading to real-world results.
  - Deep learning is driving cutting-edge progress in many industries.

## Everyday AI Applications

- **Phones:** Face unlock, voice recognition, photo tagging
- **Retail:** Stores like Amazon Go use AI for checkout-free shopping
- **Smart Homes:** Voice assistants control music, lights, and more

## AI Across Industries

- **Healthcare:**  
  - Medical imaging, drug discovery (e.g., Pfizer & IBM Watson), patient care, sensory aids for disabilities

- **Industrial:**  
  - Factory automation, predictive maintenance, agricultural optimization, inventory and delivery tracking

- **Finance:**  
  - Algorithmic trading, fraud detection, risk mitigation, personal finance management

- **Energy:**  
  - Resource discovery, smart grids, operational efficiency, energy conservation

- **Government:**  
  - Threat detection, disaster response, citizen engagement, smart city infrastructure (traffic, healthcare, emergency services)

- **Transportation:**  
  - Autonomous vehicles, automated trucking, aerospace optimization, route planning, search and rescue with AI-powered drones

- **Education:**  
  - Personalized learning and curriculum development

- **Advertising & Media:**  
  - Personalized ads, content recommendations, automated customer service

- **Sports:**  
  - Advanced analytics for recruiting, ticket pricing, and business operations

## Summary

- AI is now deeply integrated into nearly every industry, driving efficiency, personalization, and innovation.
- The combination of big data, powerful hardware, and deep learning breakthroughs makes this era of AI fundamentally different from previous waves.
- AI is often compared to electricity in its transformative impact across society.
